The **Live** page is made for playing: big tiles, one per footswitch, the preset and scene in
large type, and the tempo.

<Clip name="live" label="Stomp switches toggled, scenes Rhythm, Lead and Chorus recalled, the tempo tapped to 95 BPM" caption="Stomp mode, then Scenes mode, then tap tempo." />

Tapping a tile does what the footswitch does. The mode is chosen with **Scenes / Stomp /
Setlist** and kept across restarts.

## Stomp

<Shot src={stomp} alt="Stomp mode: Boss Metal Zone, Marshall JCM800, Mesa Boogie cabinet, Synthetic hall, BOSS CE-2w and Simple Amplifier on switches 1 to 6" caption="Stomp: each switch turns a block on or off; its LED takes the block's colour." />

Each switch turns a block on or off. By default switch 1 is the first block of the chain, switch
2 the second, and so on. To choose, tap **Assign**, then a tile, then a block, or **Automatic**
to go back to the chain order. Assignments are saved in the preset.

## Scenes

<Shot src={scenes} alt="Scenes mode: Rhythm, Lead, Chorus and Dry, scenes 5 to 8 empty" caption="Scenes: the current one is lit in amber. Rename names it." />

Each switch recalls a scene of the preset. Selecting an empty one copies the current scene into
it. See [scenes](../presets/#scenes).

## Setlist

<Shot src={setlist} alt="Setlist mode with the Friday gig setlist, Thrash loaded on switch 2" caption="Setlist: each switch loads a preset of the setlist." />

Each switch loads a preset of the current setlist. **Setlists** opens the list to choose another
one.

## Tempo

Each preset has a tempo, from 40 to 240 BPM. Set it with **TAP** (the average of your last four
taps), or with **−** and **+**. The tempo is sent to the plugins that have a tempo control and to
those that follow the host's tempo, so delays stay in time. A [MIDI clock](../midi/#clock) can
set it too; the Live page then shows **MIDI CLOCK**.

## Footswitches

With footswitches wired (see [Footswitches and encoder](../../setup/footswitches/)), each one does
what its tile does:

- Switches act when **pressed**, not on release, so a change lands on the beat.
- With two switches or more, the **last** one taps the tempo instead. **Held** for 0.8 s, it opens
  the tuner; pressing any switch then goes back. This can be turned off in Settings → Hardware.
- On a USB keyboard, `1` to `8` are the switches and `t` taps the tempo.
- On MIDI, CC49 to CC56 are switches 1 to 8.

## With the encoder

With a [rotary encoder](../../setup/footswitches/#the-encoder), turning goes through the
switches, then the tempo, **TAP** and the mode.

- On a switch, a press acts like the footswitch.
- On the tempo, press, turn to change it by 1 BPM per click, then press to keep it or hold to go
  back to the tempo before.
- On the mode, each press goes to the next one: Scenes, Stomp, Setlist.

## Tails

Delays and reverbs keep ringing when they are bypassed: their input fades out, their output
plays on until it is silent. Tails carry over scene changes and stomps. A preset change or
adding a block crossfades to a new chain in 20 ms, which ends them. Other blocks are bypassed
with a 10 ms ramp, so there is no click.
