OpenRig has three levels, as on most floor units:

| Level | What it holds | Switching |
|---|---|---|
| **Preset** | The whole chain: blocks, splits, settings, colours, its 8 scenes and its tempo | 20 ms crossfade |
| **Scene** | Inside a preset: which blocks are on, their settings, and the splits' settings | Instant, delays and reverbs ring on |
| **Setlist** | Up to 8 presets for a gig, all loaded in memory | 20 ms crossfade, no disk access |

## Presets

The **Presets** page lists your presets. Tap one to load it; the one playing is marked
**LOADED**.

<Shot src={presets} alt="The Presets page with five presets, Thrash loaded" caption="Presets: Save writes over the current preset, Save as new keeps it and makes a copy." />

- **Save** writes the current chain into the current preset.
- **Save as new** asks for a name on the on-screen keyboard and makes a new preset.
- The bin deletes a preset, after asking.

You do not lose anything by not saving: the current chain is kept across restarts. Saving is
how you go back to a sound later.

A preset keeps the settings of an [LV2 plugin](../../setup/lv2/) by the name of each control,
so an update of the plugin that adds or reorders controls keeps your sound. A preset that uses
a plugin this pedalboard does not have (made on another one, or after a removal) still loads
the rest of its chain, and saving again keeps that plugin's settings for when it is back.

## Scenes

A preset has 8 scenes. A scene remembers, for every block, whether it is on and its settings,
and for every split its A/B side, mix and pans. It does not add or remove blocks: the chain is
the same in every scene of the preset.

- The current scene is always being edited: what you change in the rack stays with the scene
  where you changed it.
- Selecting an empty scene copies the current one into it. Build the verse, switch to scene 2,
  turn the drive on: that is the chorus.
- A scene change only sends the differences to the audio, without rebuilding the chain, so
  delays and reverbs keep ringing across it.

Scenes are switched and renamed on the [Live page](../live/). The top bar shows the current
scene in amber above the preset name.

## Setlists

A setlist puts up to 8 presets on the footswitches, in the order of your set. Loading it loads
every preset in memory, so changing song is instant.

<Shot src={setlists} alt="The Setlists tab with the Friday gig setlist, active, holding five presets" caption="Setlists: New setlist asks for a name; the active one is marked." />

On the **Slots** tab, tap a slot to put a preset on it, or the cross to clear it.

<Shot src={slots} alt="The Slots tab: Hard Rock, Thrash, Metal Zone, Heavy Lead and Doom Riffs on footswitches 1 to 5" caption="The slots of a setlist, one per footswitch." />

Then play it from the Live page in **Setlist** mode, from the footswitches, or with MIDI program
changes.
