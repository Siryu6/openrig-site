The rack is the main page: the guitar comes in at **IN** on the left and leaves at **OUT** on
the right, through every block in between.

<Shot src={rack} alt="A chain with a Boss Metal Zone, a Peavey 5150, a Mesa cabinet, a hall reverb, then a Mix split with a chorus and a second amp" caption="Blocks are drawn after their gear: a pedal, an amp head, a cabinet, a reverb, and a Mix split." />

Blocks are drawn after the gear they stand for (amp head, combo, cabinet, pedal, reverb) and
coloured by family: amps red, cabinets orange, drives yellow, reverbs teal, delays green,
modulation blue. The LED of a block is lit when it is on. Blocks playing a TONE3000 capture
carry its mark.

## Adding a block

Tap **+** at the top right. The list shows the built-in blocks first, then the LV2 plugins,
sorted by category. Tap a line to add it at the end of the chain, then hold and drag it where
you want it.

**STORE**, on the same page, opens the [plugin store](../../setup/store/) to install more plugins.

<Shot src={addEffect} alt="The Add effect page, with categories and the Amp, Amp + Cab, Pedal, Cabinet and Space blocks" caption="Add effect: built-in blocks first, then every LV2 plugin found, by category." />

## Quick knobs

Tap a block to select it. Its controls show along the bottom of the screen, five at a time; the
arrows on the right show the next ones. The buttons on the left turn it on or off, or open the
full [editor](../editor/).

- **Knobs** turn with a vertical drag. **Double tap** a knob to put it back to its default
  value. **Hold** it without moving to go back to the value before your last change, to compare
  the two; hold again to swap back.
- **Switches** toggle with a tap.
- **Lists** open just above the strip, next to the field: tap an option to pick it. The current
  one is marked. Tap anywhere else to close the list.

Which controls the strip shows, and in which order, is set with
[Arrange](../editor/#arrange) in the editor.

<Shot src={strip} alt="The Peavey 5150 block selected with Input, Output, Normalize, Gate and Threshold along the bottom" caption="A selected amp: Input, Output, Normalize, Gate and Threshold; page 2 has the tone stack." />

Every control has its field: knobs, switches, and lists, which open just above the strip so
they stay near the finger. After the controls come two more fields:

- **Presets**: the plugin's factory settings, when it has any. Pick one to load it.
- **The model** of a neural, impulse response or file block (Amp, Cabinet, Sample…): the other
  presets of the same tone to switch to, and **Choose another…** to open the full
  [model chooser](../editor/#model). With no preset to list, a tap opens the chooser.

[Arrange](../editor/#arrange) sets which controls the strip shows and in what order.

## Bypass and move

- **Hold** a block and release it in place to bypass it. Delays and reverbs keep ringing (see
  [tails](../live/#tails)).
- **Hold and drag** it to move it anywhere in the chain, including into a split branch.

## Removing a block

- **Hold and drag** the block: a **Remove** target shows at the bottom of the rack. Drop the
  block on it when it turns red and says **Release to remove**.
- Or open its [editor](../editor/#header) and use the bin at the end of the header.

The rack then shows **Block removed** with **Undo** for 8 seconds. Undo puts the block back
where it was, with its settings and colour, as long as nothing else changed in between. It works
for splits too.

## Splits

The split button, next to **+**, adds an empty split at the end of the chain:

<Shot src={splitMenu} alt="The split menu: Parallel mix, A/B switch, Left / right" caption="Three kinds of split. A split's type can be changed later, its blocks are kept." />

| Split | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Parallel mix** | Both branches play, mixed together. Each can be panned in stereo mode |
| **A/B switch** | One branch plays at a time; switch it live, from a scene or from MIDI |
| **Left / right** | The top branch goes to the left output, the bottom one to the right |

Tap a split to select it: the panel at the bottom changes its type, switches A/B, sets the mix
and the pans, and adds a block to its top or bottom branch. Its bin, at the far end of the
panel, removes the split. Blocks can also be dragged into a
branch, and splits can be nested.

A typical use of **Left / right**: a cabinet impulse response on one side for the front of house,
and no cabinet on the other side for a real guitar cab on stage.

## Mono and stereo

The **Mono / Stereo** switch above the rack sets how the chain is played, and is saved in the
preset.

- **Mono** keeps the whole path mono and sends the same signal to both outputs. Use it with a
  single amp or a single output.
- **Stereo**: the guitar comes in mono and becomes stereo where something makes it so: a
  Left / right split, a panned Mix split, a stereo plugin or a stereo impulse response. After
  that point, cabinets, spaces and mono LV2 plugins run once per side. Neural blocks, too costly
  to double, play the mix of both sides.

In the rack, a single line carries a mono signal and a double line a stereo one.

With an interface that has more than two outputs or more than one input, the **Send** and
**Return** blocks (family **Routing**, at the end of the list) take the signal out of the chain
to an output, or bring another input in. See
[Inputs and outputs](../../setup/routing/#send-and-return-blocks).

## The meters

**IN** and **OUT** at the top left show the input and output levels. The red LED at the end
lights when the signal clips: lower the gain of the interface, or the output of the last blocks.
