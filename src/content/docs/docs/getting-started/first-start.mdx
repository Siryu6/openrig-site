When OpenRig starts, it shows the boot logo, then the rack. The audio is already running: the
**IN** meter at the top left moves when you play.

<Shot src={rack} alt="The rack page after start-up" caption="The rack after start-up. The IN and OUT meters show the levels; the top bar shows the DSP load." />

## 1. Check the levels

Play hard: the **IN** meter should go well into the green, without lighting the red clip LED at
its end. If it stays low or clips, set the gain on the audio interface itself.

If the meters do not move at all, see [No sound](../../help/troubleshooting/#no-sound).

## 2. Join a network

A network is only needed for [TONE3000](../../using/tone3000/) and [updates](../../setup/updates/):
the pedalboard plays without one.

Open the menu **⋮** at the top right → **Settings** → **Wi-Fi**, pick your network and type its
password on the on-screen keyboard. Ethernet works as soon as the cable is plugged in.

<Shot src={menu} alt="The menu with TONE3000, Settings and Power off" caption="The menu ⋮: TONE3000 library, Settings, Power off." />

:::tip
Set `wifi_country` in [pedalboard.txt](../../setup/pedalboard-txt/) to your country's two letters
(`FR`, `US`…) so the Wi-Fi uses the channels allowed there.
:::

## 3. Link TONE3000 (optional)

To download amp and pedal captures from [tone3000.com](https://www.tone3000.com), link your
account in **Settings** → **TONE3000 Connect**. It takes a minute: see
[TONE3000](../../using/tone3000/).

<Shot src={settings2} alt="Settings: Wi-Fi, TONE3000 Connect and Updates" caption="Settings, further down: Wi-Fi, TONE3000 Connect and Updates." />

## 4. Build a sound

Tap **+** at the top right of the rack to add a block, starting with an **Amp + Cab**, or an
**Amp** followed by a **Cabinet**. Then read the [tour of the screen](../../using/tour/).

## Switching off

Use **⋮** → **Power off**: the pedalboard saves and switches off cleanly. Pulling the power is
safe too: the system is read-only and your chain is saved a few seconds after each change.
